About these statistics

  • Reliability. Cronbach's alpha is recalculated for the selected students. For all 60 it is 0.73, which is acceptable for a four-dimension rubric. It shows whether the dimensions move together, not whether raters agree.
  • Dimension differences. For all 60 students, Systems Reasoning is significantly lower than Stakeholder Analysis and Recommendation (paired Wilcoxon tests, Holm-adjusted p < .05).
  • Section differences. None are statistically significant (Kruskal–Wallis p > .20 for every dimension). With 15 students per section, treat gaps between sections as something to watch.
  • Target. The program has not set a benchmark. The 80% line is illustrative; change TARGET in app.js to match your program's target.
